## Recommended plan

1. **Remove pages:** Press, Careers, Status, Affiliate (and their menu/footer links).
2. **Trim the home page to 7–8 sections:** Banner → Categories → Courses → About (online + in-person) → Why Choose Us → Instructors → Testimonials → Contact CTA. Drop the marquees, fake video controls, clipart and duplicate sections.
3. **Clean the data:** delete the test course and user, replace "Demo Instructor", hide the demo logins, and use real instructor photos or initials.
4. **Real facts only:** one address (Lahore), real student and course numbers (or none), PKR prices, real phone/WhatsApp, real social links, "EduPortal by XtroEdge" in the footer.
5. **Make forms work** (save to the database or send email), or remove them.
6. **Security:** remove the Admin role from public sign-up; disable the mock Google login until real OAuth is set up.
7. **Later:** move the site from `/site` to `/` and retire the old public site.

## Details needed from XtroEdge

- Official address(es) of the campus(es)
- Phone / WhatsApp number and support hours
- Real student, course and instructor numbers
- Course prices in PKR
- Social media links (Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube…)
- Official email domain
- Real instructor names and photos, and real testimonials (with permission)
